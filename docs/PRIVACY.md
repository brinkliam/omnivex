# Omnivex — Privacy Policy (draft)

*This is a draft for the store listing. Have it reviewed before release, and update it when real ad and analytics SDKs are added.*

**What the game stores on your device:** your progress, settings and purchases. These stay on your device.

**Anonymous gameplay statistics (optional):** if you agree on first launch, the game records anonymous events. Examples are how far a run got, which mutations were picked, and which optional reward videos were watched. These are used only to balance the game. They contain no name, email, contacts, precise location or advertising identifier, and the session id is random each launch. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

**Advertising (planned):** release builds will show ads through Unity LevelPlay and its ad partners. Those partners may use device identifiers to measure and personalise ads, subject to your consent choices and platform settings. Before personalised ads are shown, you'll be asked through the platform's consent dialog where the law requires it. Ad-partner policies will be linked here at release.

**Purchases:** handled by Google Play or the App Store. The game never sees your payment details.

**Children:** Omnivex is not directed at children under 13.

**Contact:** to be added by the publisher before release.
